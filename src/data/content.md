## **GNSS / GPS Surveying**

Our GNSS/GPS surveying services provide high-accuracy positioning for large-scale projects and geodetic control networks. Using advanced satellite-based technology, we are able to capture precise coordinates over extensive areas with efficiency and reliability.

This method is ideal for establishing control points, mapping large sites, and supporting infrastructure development. By leveraging real-time kinematic (RTK) and post-processing techniques, we ensure centimeter-level accuracy that meets engineering and construction standards.

GNSS surveying is especially effective in open environments where speed and coverage are essential, allowing us to deliver accurate geospatial data with reduced field time.

---

## **Total Station Surveying**

Total station surveying delivers highly precise measurements for detailed mapping and construction layout. By combining electronic distance measurement with angular observations, this method ensures exceptional accuracy for site-specific work.

We use total stations for:

* Construction staking and layout  
* Boundary and detail surveys  
* Structural positioning and alignment

This approach is essential for projects that require tight tolerances and exact positioning, such as building foundations, road alignments, and utility installations. Our expertise ensures that every point is measured and placed with precision.

## **Drone (UAV) Surveys**

Our drone surveying services provide a fast, safe, and cost-effective solution for capturing high-resolution aerial data. Using unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), we can efficiently survey large or inaccessible areas while maintaining exceptional detail and accuracy.

Drone surveys significantly reduce the need for manual fieldwork and improve safety by eliminating the need to access hazardous terrain. The collected imagery is processed into accurate geospatial products such as orthophotos, 3D models, and elevation datasets.

This service is ideal for land development, construction monitoring, infrastructure projects, and environmental analysis.

## **Construction & As-Built Surveys**

We provide construction and as-built surveying services to ensure that projects are executed according to design specifications and documented accurately upon completion.

**Construction layout surveying** involves setting out precise locations for structures, utilities, and infrastructure elements based on engineering plans. This ensures that all components are built in the correct position and alignment.

**As-built surveys** are conducted after construction to verify and document the final condition of the project. These surveys identify any deviations from the original design and provide accurate records for future maintenance, compliance, and project validation.

Our services help minimize errors, reduce costly rework, and ensure that projects meet both technical and regulatory requirements.

## **LiDAR Data Processing**

Our LiDAR data processing services transform raw laser scanning data into highly accurate and structured geospatial information. Using specialized tools such as Bentley TerraScan, we analyze and refine large point cloud datasets to extract meaningful insights.

We perform:

* Point cloud **classification** (ground, vegetation, buildings, utilities)  
* **Filtering and noise removal**  
* Generation of **Digital Terrain Models (DTM)**  
* Feature extraction and contour creation

LiDAR technology is especially valuable for projects requiring precise elevation data, including areas with dense vegetation or complex terrain. Our processing ensures clean, reliable datasets ready for engineering and GIS applications.
